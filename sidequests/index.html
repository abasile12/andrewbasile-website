Wordchainer

Live now at wordchainer.fun

A daily word game you can finish before the coffee cools.

Wordchainer started as the TikTok word chain game my friends and I kept playing out loud, so I built it into something you could play alone. Every day there is one public chain: you get the first word, the last word and the first letter of everything in between, and you guess your way across. Share your time, build a chain for a friend, or race someone head to head.

soundlog.io

Moneyball for music.

SoundLog took the audio feature metrics Spotify attaches to every song (key, tempo, energy, danceability and more) and turned them into insights the industry could act on. The kind of thing you could not unsee once you saw it: in 2020, 90% of the songs on RapCaviar were in C#. It grew into a catalog management and intelligence tool for publishers and labels, with a songwriter spotlight series on the side.
